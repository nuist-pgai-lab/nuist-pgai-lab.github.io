Join PGAI Lab at NUIST

Systematic training, visible outcomes and a warm, supportive community. We are recruiting master's students (academic and professional tracks) for 2026. Excellent students are warmly welcome to apply!

Email the Admissions Contact Meet Our Faculty

What You Will Get

① Systematic Training

  • Research fundamentals: literature, algorithms, experiments, writing
  • AI skills: mathematics, algorithm principles, engineering practice
  • Hands-on projects: funded research and industry collaborations
  • Academic communication: group meetings and conference presentations

② Visible Outcomes

  • Papers & patents: top-quartile (Q1) papers, CCF-recommended venues
  • Awards: national/provincial competitions and innovation projects
  • Academic exchange: presentations at well-known conferences
  • Internship recommendations for qualified students

③ Where Graduates Go

  • Further study: USTC, Beihang, Tongji University, and more
  • Employers: Baidu, Bank of China, renowned research institutes
  • Industry referrals: Tencent, Baidu, Alibaba, miHoYo, etc.
  • Academic referrals: HIT, USTC, WHU, UBC, etc.

④ A Supportive Community

  • Advisor guidance: regular group meetings or one-on-one supervision
  • Peer support: paper revision, debugging, experiment assistance
  • Resources: ample AI compute, LLM tools, lab equipment
  • Team culture: team-building events and holiday celebrations

How to Apply

Please apply for the master's program (academic or professional track) at the School of Artificial Intelligence, Nanjing University of Information Science and Technology (NUIST). The number of positions per advisor follows the school's annual admission plan (see individual advisor pages under People).

We encourage you to contact your preferred advisor by email in advance, introducing your academic background, research interests and project experience.
