People

Director

Feng Jiang
Professor · PhD Supervisor · Dean · Lab Director
Homepage ↗

Dean of the School of Artificial Intelligence (School of Future Technology). Second-level fellow of the Jiangsu “333 High-Level Talents Program” (2024) and listed among the Elsevier/Stanford Top 2% Scientists worldwide (2024).

His research spans AI, computer vision and intelligent human-machine engineering. He has published 200+ papers (40+ as first author; most-cited paper 400+ citations) in JMLR, IEEE TIP/TCSVT/TMM, CVPR, AAAI and ACM MM, authored 4 monographs/textbooks and holds 30+ patents. He has led 30+ projects including National Key R&D Program topics and NSFC grants. His first-of-a-kind systems for healthcare and industry are deployed in 15 hospitals, 6 large state-owned enterprises and 20+ companies, earning the Heilongjiang Provincial Science & Technology Progress Second Prize (ranked 1st) among other awards. He chairs the IEEE Signal Processing Society Harbin Chapter, serves as Associate Editor of Springer's Journal of Signal Processing Systems, and is an Honorary Professor at the University of Nottingham.

Email: fjiang@nuist.edu.cn
