People

Director

JF
Feng Jiang
Professor · PhD Supervisor · Lab Director

Executive Vice Dean of the School of Artificial Intelligence. Second-level fellow of the Jiangsu Province “333 High-Level Talents Program”, and listed among the Elsevier/Stanford Top 2% Scientists worldwide.

His research spans artificial intelligence, computer vision and intelligent equipment. He has published 200+ papers, authored 4 monographs, and holds 30+ patents. He has led 30+ research projects including the National Key R&D Program and NSFC grants. His first-of-a-kind systems for healthcare and industry have been deployed in 15 hospitals, 6 large state-owned enterprises and 20+ companies, earning multiple provincial/ministerial science and technology awards.

Research: Artificial Intelligence, Computer Vision, Intelligent Human-Machine Engineering

Email: fjiang@nuist.edu.cn
