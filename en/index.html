About the Lab

The Perceptual and Generative AI Lab (PGAI Lab) belongs to the School of Artificial Intelligence (School of Future Technology) at Nanjing University of Information Science and Technology (NUIST). The lab has 12 full-time faculty members with backgrounds in AI, computer science, communications, statistics and photogrammetry, including 2 national/provincial-level talents and 1 Elsevier/Stanford Top 2% Scientist. Our research spans generative foundation models, object detection and recognition, 2D/3D reconstruction, perception and interaction, image quality assessment, depth estimation, and AI for weather and healthcare, following an academia–engineering dual-drive model. Over the past decade we have published 337 papers (including IEEE TPAMI, CVPR, ICLR and AAAI), authored 6 monographs and textbooks, been granted 30+ invention patents, and led 30+ projects including National Key R&D Program topics and NSFC grants, with deployments in healthcare, industry and meteorology.

Research Areas

Frontier AI research with pathways to real-world applications

Generative Foundation Models

Probabilistic generative models, diffusion & controllable image generation

Object Detection & Recognition

Infrared small targets, coastal targets & industrial defect inspection

2D/3D Reconstruction

Multi-source remote sensing, panoramic imagery & point-cloud reconstruction

Perception & Interaction

Human motion perception, human-machine interaction & intelligent equipment

Image Quality Assessment

Visual quality assessment, compressive sensing & image reconstruction

Depth Estimation

Monocular / omnidirectional depth estimation & 3D perception

AI for Weather

Smart meteorology, multi-source fusion & intelligent forecasting

AI for Healthcare

Medical image analysis, smart healthcare & health computing

Our research extends to applications in autonomous driving, video surveillance, industrial inspection and robotics.
